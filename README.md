## 🌟 What is Ant-cestry Pro?

Every day, billions of ants walk past each other on kitchen countertops, sidewalks, and picnic blankets without knowing their lineage. Are they 3rd cousins? Are they scouting for rival queens?

**Ant-cestry Pro** solves this non-existent problem using playful visual tracking, interactive simulation, and chaotic sensory feedback.

---

## ✨ Ridiculous & Fun Features

### 1. 🔬 Ant Pedigree Scanner (Camera Viewfinder)
- Point your rear or front camera at any countertop ant (or use the simulated bug viewfinder).
- Real-time targeting reticle tracks ant coordinates.
- Generates an immediate lineage breakdown:
  - **73% Red Harvester**
  - **19% Carpenter Ant**
  - **8% Sugar Raider**
  - **0% Cousin Guarantee** (certified non-incestuous colony connection)

### 2. 🌌 Zero-G Party Mode
- Hit the **"Zero-G Party"** button in the header to suspend gravitational laws.
- All cards, headers, navigation pills, and crawling ants detach and float weightlessly in space with gentle rotation, bobbing physics, and retro synth arpeggios.
- Click **"Stop Party"** and gravity abruptly slams everything back into the ground with an elastic thud.

### 3. 🚨 Pheromone Glitch & Olfactory Nose Tracker (< 4 cm)
- Tap **"Emit Pheromones!"** in the Pheromone tab.
- **Screen Quake**: The entire screen begins to vibrate and violently shake (`@keyframes screenQuake`).
- **Chemical Glitch**: Text morphs into chaotic chemical glyphs (`[FORMIC]`, `[CH4]`, `[VAPOR]`).
- **Nose Cleansing Required**: Manual clicks and taps are blocked. To stop the shaking and restore human English, you **must bring the tip of your nose within < 4 cm of the front camera lens** to "inhale" the formic vapor.

### 4. 🐜 Interactive Screen Ants
- Vector ants continuously crawl across your viewport with animated leg-wiggling mechanics.
- **Tap to Panic**: Poke any ant to send it scurrying across the screen in sheer terror.
- Hit **"+ More Ants"** to invite additional colony members.

### 5. 💘 Colony Mingle
- The premiere insect dating radar.
- Browse ant profiles within pheromone scent radius (e.g., *"Barnaby (Worker #4092) - 0.4 mm away"*).
- Integrated cousin-detection warning system.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Frontend**: Pure Vanilla HTML5, TailwindCSS (CDN), Custom Cartoon Pop Art CSS.
- **Computer Vision & Sensors**: WebRTC `getUserMedia`, HTML5 Canvas chromaticity skin/nose tracking.
- **Audio**: Web Audio API Procedural Synthesizer (Zero audio asset dependencies).
- **Hosting**: Zero-build static bundle ready for Vercel, Netlify, or GitHub Pages.

## 🚀 1-Click Deployment to Vercel

1. Fork or import this repository into [Vercel](https://vercel.com/new).
2. Framework Preset: **Other** (Root directory `./`).
3. Click **Deploy**.
4. That's it! Your site will be live on `https://<your-project>.vercel.app`.
